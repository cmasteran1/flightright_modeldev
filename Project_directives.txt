This project's goal is to develop accurate commercial flight departure and arrival delay predictions for US 
domestic flights. This should be achieved through existing python based machine learning libraries. All features
must be available at least a few days before the scheduled departure time of the flight. The training data features must
be obtained from free publicly available data. If that data is not also free a few days before the flight, it must
be obtainable from the FlightLabs API endpoints.

Our target is to be able to say with confidence that a flight has a W% chance of being less than 15 minutes delayed
an X% chance of being delayed by at least 15 minutes, a Y% chance of 
being delayed by at least 30 minutes, a Z% chance of being delayed by at least 45 minutes, and an A% chance
of being delayed by over 60 minutes for both arrival and departure delays.
